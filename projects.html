PROJECTS / HOW TO BEGIN

Start with what you want to accomplish.

A project can involve research, AI, data, technology, engineering, experimentation—or a combination. You don't need to decide that in advance.

A project grows around the objective.

First, tell us what you are trying to accomplish, what success might look like and what you already know. Odrusmark reviews the request and follows up to discuss fit, questions, scope, timing and next steps. Work is only agreed after those details are clear.

01 · Explain the goal

Describe the change, question, opportunity or problem in your own words.

02 · Explore the possibilities

Consider context, constraints and which kinds of investigation or building may help.

03 · Agree what comes next

Confirm a practical scope and proposed next step together before work begins.

A project workspace currently supports the objective, background, status and timeline details provided at intake. Research workspaces, tasks, uploads, findings, deliverables and project messaging are future capabilities and are not represented as active features.
